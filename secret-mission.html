TUTORIAL

  1. Click the flashlight at the top right of any main page.
  2. Your mouse becomes a light. Move it around to search.
  3. Each main page hides 1–2 artifacts. Click a revealed object to collect it.
  4. Collect all 12 objects and use their clues to solve the mystery.

Press Esc to turn the flashlight off.

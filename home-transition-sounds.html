Home page / transition sound

Three smoother
stripe sounds.

Each option follows the real stripe animation: the sound swells as the three stripes speed up through the middle of their slide, and eases off as they settle. Press Play with transition to hear it in sync, or Sound only to hear it on its own. All four are set to the same loudness.

CURRENT

Today's sound

The sound on the site now, for comparison. Hiss plus a low hum.

OPTION A

Soft chord

One warm tone per stripe, arriving 45ms apart, just like the stripes. Together they form a gentle major chord that rises slightly as the stripes travel. The most musical of the three.

OPTION B

Air swoosh

A soft, low whoosh of air that brightens as the stripes cross the screen and fades as they land. Closest in spirit to today's sound, without the rasp.

OPTION C

Tape glide

A single smooth tone that glides up an octave in step with the stripes, like a tape reel spinning up, then three soft taps as each stripe lands.

On the real site the sound plays as you leave Home, and the stripes finish sliding away once the next page loads. These previews run both halves back to back.

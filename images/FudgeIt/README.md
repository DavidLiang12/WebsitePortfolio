# Fudge It! image sources

The CD cover is the supplied `Group 31.png`, converted losslessly to WebP with its transparency and 880 × 880 dimensions preserved.

The other images were downloaded as original embedded images from the supplied [Fudge It! presentation](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1US20CJOk-VQRhcEv21ltstkk5P5PJcyl1HYrhSlvrn8/edit). They are local files, with no dependency on expiring Google image URLs. Photographs and screenshots were scaled proportionally to at most 1800 pixels; card artwork was converted losslessly.

Captions also draw on the supplied design journals: [Week 1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OEau7JbitxQDli2zz1aUysNwEyACQCXNHVzEV0lID4w/edit), [Week 2](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1H-1hN_YMWUzDWVPK2Qznup4F80AsM8eVfVX5q6zG7Rk/edit).

The `candy-*.webp` decorations are the eight original transparent candy illustrations from slides 1 and 21, converted losslessly. The page's dough `#f5ce95` and frosting `#f5749f` colors are the most frequent corresponding base colors sampled from the supplied CD.

Additional process images come from those journals: `early-design-notes.webp` from Week 1; `physical-paper-prototype.webp`, `objective-prototype-sheet.webp`, `card-art-process.webp`, and `token-printing-test.webp` from Week 2. They retain the full source image and its aspect ratio.

`survey-comparison.webp` is a high-resolution extraction of the original chart on presentation slide 11. Axis labels, all ten survey items, values, and the separate-sample note are preserved. The chart was not redrawn or its data changed.

`documents/fudge-it-rulebook.pdf` is the exact supplied `FUDGE IT!.pdf`, linked from the project page as the rulebook.

`candy-box-shopping.webp` and the refreshed `token-printing-test.webp` were downloaded from the two final process photos in the Week 2 journal on November 16, 2026. Originals remain uncropped in these optimized WebP assets; the project page crops them into matching frames.

`candyland-background.svg` supplies the full-width ground silhouettes; `candyland-sky.svg` and `candyland-clouds-right.svg` supply level clouds in inset columns. The ground keeps a minimum 1280-pixel drawing width, cropping on narrow screens rather than compressing its curves. Clouds are positioned outside the ground’s vertical range.

`physical-components-photo.webp` is the full, opaque photograph supplied as `IMG_0017.jpg`, optimized for the first project image.

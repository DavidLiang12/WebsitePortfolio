About the Game

Flying Home follows a lost pigeon on a journey through changing landscapes. Dodge obstacles, collect coins, and navigate the dangers along the way home.

02

A Journey Through Changing Skies

The journey moves from city skylines to green forests, sandy deserts, and pink mountain peaks. Each setting brings a different palette and new obstacles into the pigeon’s path.

03

Under the Northern Lights

A night sky filled with auroras sets the scene for a festive landscape of decorated trees, reindeer, and candy canes.
